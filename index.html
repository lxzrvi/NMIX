Theme, dark mode and font settings are saved on this device.

Calculator

Open Calculator and use the NMIX keypad. The top fields are display-only, so your phone keyboard stays closed.

Operators

Choose +, −, ×, ÷ or %. After selecting an operator, buttons enter the second number.

Editing

Use decimal, ±, ⌫ and AC to edit or clear calculations.

Result

Press = or tap the large NMIX display when the expression is ready.

Timer

Tap Timer to open it. Use − / + for five seconds and hold Timer to start or pause.

Clock

Tap Clock for local time. Use ⛶ inside the Clock button for full screen.

Stopwatch

Tap to start or pause. Hold Stopwatch to reset.

Counters

Add and Minus change by one. Hold them for rapid counting.

Random

Tap Random for 1–1000. Hold it for rapid random values.

Top Screen

Use the top-left arrow to hide/show the NMIX screen. Opening a tool automatically restores it.

Settings

Use the hamburger for dark mode, five colors and five fonts.
